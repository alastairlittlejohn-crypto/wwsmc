Before you arrive

Getting around the building

Lift & wheelchair access

The building offers excellent disabled access, with a lift connecting to Level 1 and accessible parking bays close to the entrance.

On-site parking

A large on-site car park with entry off Edward Street serves the centre, including plenty of dedicated disabled parking.

Building hours

The building is accessible 7:00am–6:00pm, Monday to Friday. Individual specialist rooms set their own consulting days — check when you book.

Referrals & visiting

Good to know before you book

Do I need a referral?

Yes. Every specialist in this building requires a current referral letter from your GP or another treating doctor before your first consultation, in line with Medicare requirements.

Do you handle bookings for me?

No — this building houses independent specialist practices, each with their own reception and booking system. Use the phone number listed against the specialty you need in the directory above.

Is there somewhere to wait between appointments?

Each suite has its own waiting room. There's also a small shared seating area near the Ground Floor lift for those attending back-to-back appointments.

I'm not sure which specialist I need — who do I ask?

Your GP determines the referral, but if you've lost details of who you were referred to, Ground Floor reception can look up which suite your referral was sent to.
